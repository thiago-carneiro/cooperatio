# Brief: Guide to preparing a production cell

Canon: [02-canon.json](02-canon.json)

Languages: en

Target: a guide of 2,500 to 3,500 words that tells a newcomer how to prepare a production cell: the brief, the sources of truth, the canon and the artifact graph.
Audience: a teammate who has read the principle once and has never prepared a cell.

## Non-negotiables

- `nn-source`: every statement about the method can be checked in the principle; every statement about a registered prior carries its mark and can be checked in its method document.
- `nn-terms`: the terms of the canon are used in the canon's sense.
- `nn-form`: plain Markdown, no HTML, no absolute path, no authoring date, no machine name.
